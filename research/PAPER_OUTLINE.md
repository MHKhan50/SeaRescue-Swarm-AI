# Paper outline
**Communication-Aware Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Intelligence for UAV–USV Cooperative Maritime Search and Rescue**
- Civilian maritime SAR motivation and related work
- Reproducible 2D simulation, agent abstractions and victim distributions
- Baselines: independent sweep, shared assignment, communication-aware heuristic
- Experiments across communication reliability, team size and random seeds
- Metrics: coverage, discovery rate, steps-to-discovery, energy proxy
- Confidence intervals, limitations, ethics and reproducibility
